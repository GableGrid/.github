# GableGrid

Business systems, automation, data and AI for small and mid-sized businesses.
Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, working with clients worldwide.

## What we do

- **Business systems:** ERP, CRM, online stores and websites (Odoo, ERPNext, WooCommerce, WordPress)
- **Integration and automation:** systems that pass work to each other (n8n, Power Automate, APIs)
- **Data and analytics:** one data warehouse and reports people trust (PostgreSQL, Power BI)
- **AI agents:** assistants that answer and act on your own information (Dify, leading AI models)
- **Custom internal tools:** simple apps when nothing ready-made fits
